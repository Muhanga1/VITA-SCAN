Offline AI for smallholder farmers in Rwanda.

A farmer takes a photo of a sick leaf. The app works fully offline, on a low-cost phone, and explains in Kinyarwanda by voice and text: disease, medicine, dose, and fair market price.

Problem
Farmers lose harvest because they cannot detect crop disease early. No internet, no agronomist nearby, and information is in English.

Solution
- Small on-device image model under 1B parameters
- Works fully offline, no internet, no cloud
- Voice-first in Kinyarwanda for low-literacy farmers
- One scan gives: disease + medicine + dose + market price

Crops
Maize, beans, potato, cassava

Status
In use by farmers and cooperatives in Rwanda.
